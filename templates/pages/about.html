ABOUT AGRICONNECT

Connecting Uganda's Agriculture Community

AgriConnect is a marketplace concept designed to help farmers, traders, retailers, and food businesses discover opportunities and share agricultural market information.

Our Purpose

Make it easier for farmers and buyers to discover one another and discuss produce availability and prices.

Who We Serve

Farmers, produce traders, retailers, restaurants, and food businesses across Uganda.

What We Provide

Sample product listings, market-price displays, and a starting point for direct buyer-farmer connections.
